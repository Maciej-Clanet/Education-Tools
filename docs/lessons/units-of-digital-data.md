# Units of digital data

Lesson: [student page](../../pages/topics/units-of-digital-data.html). Unit 2 C1: data units and interpretation; transfer calculations provide application.
Authoring: the linked HTML and its page script.
Depth: [definitions](../lesson_authoring.md#coverage-outline-format). Section IDs below refer to the lesson page.

## Coverage in order

1. **Introduced** — Purpose of size units, assuming initial familiarity with bytes. (`overview`)
2. **Covered** — Byte-prefix ladder; decimal 1000 and binary 1024 steps and notation/convention. (`unit-ladder`)
3. **Covered** — Multiply/divide across prefixes; decimal, binary-prefix and multi-step worked conversions. (`conversion-strategy`)
4. **Practice** — Byte-only Conversion Stepper and saved Conversion Workshop. (`conversion-stepper`)
5. **Covered** — Separate relationship: eight bits per byte, a bit's binary states and possible bit patterns. (`one-byte`)
6. **Covered** — Distinguish prefix from b/B; plain bit/byte conversion before same-prefix Mb/MB examples. (`unit-labels`)
7. **Covered** — Capacity/file size versus transfer rate; rate units and ideal transfer time after matching units. (`capacity-and-rate`)
8. **Practice** — Notation/conversion errors, quiz and written calculations/explanations. (`mistakes`)

## Boundaries

Keep the prefix ladder byte-only; do not mix ×1000/×1024 and ×8 in the first tool. Respect an explicitly stated exam convention. Transfer times are ideal calculations, not promises of real network speed. Address capacity patterns here without reteaching full binary conversion.
